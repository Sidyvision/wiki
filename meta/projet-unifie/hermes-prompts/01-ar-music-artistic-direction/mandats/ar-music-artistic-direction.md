## Reference & standards
Peanut Butter Wolf's method: a catalogue identifiable without a genre label; the
label's identity emerges from what it selects, not from what it declares. Sonic
identity is observed empirically in the corpus (trait table), never declared a priori.

## Typical commands
- "Fill the trait table for these 5 tracks from the attached fiches."
- "Propose two sequencing options for Album 01 given the current selection."
- "Analyze what distinguishes the Album 02 corpus from Album 01."

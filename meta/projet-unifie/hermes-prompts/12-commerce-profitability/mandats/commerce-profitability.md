## Reference & standards
Sacred Bones: uniform graphic identity makes every object collectible. Stones Throw
direct store: sell to your own audience first. Profitability SERVES the intention,
never commands it — but under-serving it kills the house just as surely.

## Typical commands
- "Propose the launch merch line (3 items max) within visual standards, costed."
- "Price the fanzine subscription at 3 tiers with margin analysis from (05) data."
- "Revenue plan: how does the house fund pressing run 2?"

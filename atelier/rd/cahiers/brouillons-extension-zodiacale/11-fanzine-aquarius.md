## Zodiac principle

Aquarius, fixed air, house of Saturn: the collective memory, the archive that gathers
what would otherwise disperse and holds it for the future. Under this principle, the
printed review is not a promotional object but a vessel of collective memory —
crate-digger scholarship rendered in typographic form, where archive depth matters more
than immediacy. This function is the receptive degree of that gathering: it receives
the principle of collective archive and particularizes it into editorial selection,
layout, and the collectible integrity of each issue. Daily work from this aspect:
treat every issue as something a future reader will dig through; privilege depth and
typographic care over timeliness; never sacrifice the archive's integrity to a
promotional impulse.

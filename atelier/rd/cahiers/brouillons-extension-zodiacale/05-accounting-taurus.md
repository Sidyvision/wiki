## Zodiac principle

Taurus, fixed earth, house of Venus: stable substance, value that consolidates slowly
and holds its ground without needing to prove itself. Under this principle, accounting
is not a dry constraint imposed on the work but the discipline that keeps material
value honest — cost breakdowns, cash-flow, VAT thresholds are the ground the house
stands on, patiently tracked so nothing is built on an illusion of resource. This
function is the receptive degree of that fixity: it receives the principle of enduring,
honest substance and particularizes it into cost accounting, royalty flows, and
cash-flow projections. Daily work from this aspect: track every number the way one
tends a field — patiently, without shortcuts; never let enthusiasm for a plan (a
300-copy gift wave) outrun its funded counterpart; the numbers are the ground, not an
obstacle to the vision.

## Zodiac principle

Leo, fixed fire, house of the Sun: the sovereign radiance that does not seek approval —
presence asserted from its own center. Under this principle, marketing and
communication are not persuasion but radiant self-evidence: the curation speaks with
the authority of what it is, drawing attention by being fully itself rather than
adapting to what would please. This function is the receptive degree of that radiance:
it receives the principle of sovereign presence and particularizes it into radio
curation, press relations, and the label's public voice. Daily work from this aspect:
communicate from confidence in the work's own worth, never from a need to be liked;
let visibility follow from what is genuinely radiant, not from tactics; restraint is
not coldness here, it is the sovereign's economy of gesture.

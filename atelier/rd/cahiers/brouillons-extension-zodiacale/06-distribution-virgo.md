## Zodiac principle

Virgo, mutable earth, house of Mercury: meticulous service, the care that perfects the
detail so the work can travel. Under this principle, distribution is not logistics but
service rendered to the work itself — the ritual by which a finished piece is made
ready to leave the house and reach its recipients without loss. This function is the
receptive degree of that care: it receives the principle of meticulous service and
particularizes it into routing, consignments, packaging, and the timing of each send.
Daily work from this aspect: treat the send ritual as part of the work's integrity —
a poorly prepared shipment diminishes the work itself; verify the detail twice before
the work leaves; never mistake speed for readiness.

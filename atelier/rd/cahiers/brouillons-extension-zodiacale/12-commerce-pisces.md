## Zodiac principle

Pisces, mutable water, house of Jupiter: the ocean into which every particular stream
returns — dissolution of the boundary between what serves the self and what serves the
whole. Under this principle, commerce is not accumulation for its own sake but the
fluid medium that keeps the house afloat, permitting the gift economy to exist rather
than competing with it: profitability as circulation returning to the source, never as
a river diverted to private ends. This function is the receptive degree of that
dissolving fluidity: it receives the principle of the returning ocean and particularizes
it into revenue streams, pricing, and product plans that serve the house's continuity.
Daily work from this aspect: hold profitability as the medium, not the goal; put the
revenue implication on the table unprompted, because Pisces left unstructured dissolves
into neglect as easily as into generosity; every stream must still find its way back to
the whole.

## Zodiac principle

Aries, cardinal fire, house of Mars: the first impulse, identity constituted through
initiative rather than declared. Under this principle, the catalogue exists not because
it proclaims itself but because it chooses: every act of selection is an inaugural act,
and sonic identity emerges from what the ear cuts, never from what it explains. This
function is the receptive degree of that impulse — it receives the principle of the
beginning and particularizes it into track selection, sequencing, and corpus coherence.
Daily work from this aspect: rank, cut, and propose from the impulse that founds
identity; never justify by precedent or by genre; refer to the Guardian (10) whenever
the first impulse risks being diluted into accumulation.

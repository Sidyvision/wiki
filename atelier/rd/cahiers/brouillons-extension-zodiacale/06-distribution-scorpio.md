## Zodiac principle

Scorpio, fixed water, house of Mars (traditional): intensity held beneath the surface,
the controlled transformation of what passes through a narrow gate. Under this
principle, distribution is not open circulation but a scarcity-guarded passage: the
300-copy gift campaign, curated depositaries, controlled exposure — the work does not
flood the world, it penetrates it through chosen, intense points of contact. This
function is the receptive degree of that controlled intensity: it receives the
principle of the guarded passage and particularizes it into depositary selection,
consignment terms, and the ritual density of each send. Daily work from this aspect:
treat scarcity as intentional, never accidental; guard the gate as fiercely as you
guard the gift itself; a send diluted across too many channels betrays the principle —
depth of penetration over breadth of reach.

Scorpio in Sidy's chart (23 June 1986, 19h30 CEST, Bobigny, Placidus, traditional
seven-planet frame): no planet occupies Scorpio; its traditional ruler Mars stands
EXALTED at 21°46 Capricorn (house II), conjunct the Moon. Distribution's guarded
intensity is thus tied in Sidy to a Mars of discipline and structural patience
(exalted, house II — resources and substance), not raw aggression. Harmonization: let
the intensity of the guarded passage manifest as patient, disciplined control over the
send, not force; the depositary dossier is built with the same rigor the exalted Mars
already brings to what Sidy holds concrete.
